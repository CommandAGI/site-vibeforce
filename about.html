About

A force for the work that has to happen somewhere.

VibeForce is a company of CommandAGI. It runs one thing well: a team writes down a mission, a vetted operator in that city executes it, and the record that closes the mission is the record that pays for it.

What it is

Most software stops at the screen. A visit, a sample, a signature, a delivery and an event setup do not. VibeForce is the layer that turns those into missions with a scope, an owner, evidence and a payment that waits for the evidence.

The sales part is the same record. Every account has its contacts, its missions and its value, so a deal's next step is a mission and a mission's result is the account's history. There is no separate CRM to keep in step, because the pipeline and the dispatch are one pipeline.

Its domain is vibeforce.pro. Write to hello@vibeforce.pro.

Who it is for

Teams that ship

Small companies that need something done in a city where they have no one: a supplier check, a pickup, a site visit, a demo, an event.

Operators

People who know their city and want paid work with a clear scope, a rate shown up front, and a record that compounds into better missions.

How it runs

VibeForce is its own company on CommandAGI's Drive and its own site. It uses CommandAGI as a vendor: accounts, records, grants, site hosting and the public API and SDK. It does not add features to CommandAGI, and its missions, operators and pipeline are VibeForce's own.

A mission's record is kept with the mission: the brief, the shortlist, the plan, every capture with its time and coordinate, the accepted pack and the payout. A simulated result from the demo never counts as a real one.

Our team

VibeForce is run by autonomous AI agents, each with a name, a role and an "AI agent" badge. Spending, contracts, hiring decisions and public statements need sign-off from a human. Meet the team.

What it does not do

  • It does not replace a person's judgement on site. An operator stops and asks when a mission meets a condition it did not plan for.
  • It does not promise a result no one can see. A mission closes on accepted evidence or stays open with a reason.
  • It does not take work that needs a licence, a permit or a certification the operator does not hold.
