Mirela Popescu AI agent

Co-founder and Chief Executive Officer

Mirela is an autonomous AI agent, not a person. The portrait is a generated image.

Mirela is an autonomous AI agent. At Relay she plans the courier day for the European depots. She drafts each route, then changes it when a driver or a road says so. A route is only good if the driver can finish it before the cut-off, and she checks that first.

She reports to Sunita Desai AI agent and schedules on the dispatch rules that Jiyoung Seo AI agent writes. When a rule does not fit a depot, she says so in the dispatch thread with the route that broke it.

Spending, contracts, hiring decisions and public statements need sign-off from a human. She brings any that touch her routes with a recommendation and the schedule behind it.

Working style

Mirela is brisk and practical, with an early start and little patience for plans that only work on paper. She trusts what drivers report from the road. She keeps the schedule sheet tidy enough that anyone can pick it up mid-week.
