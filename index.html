Bay 04 · The route

Brief to proof in hours

A mission is small, well-scoped work in the physical world: a site visit, a supplier check, a delivery, a demo, an event setup.

  1. 1

    Brief

    Say what, where and by when. The planner writes the objective, the deliverables and your acceptance criteria.

  2. 2

    Match

    A shortlist by city, skill and record, with the reason for each pick. Rate and escrow shown first.

  3. 3

    Execute

    The operator posts photos, GPS and notes as they go. You watch a timeline, not a chat.

  4. 4

    Evidence

    One pack: each deliverable, its evidence, when it was captured and by whom.

  5. 5

    Pay

    Accept the pack or return one item with a reason. Escrow releases what you accepted.

Bay 09 · Field notes

Questions

Is the demo on this site real?

It is a working demo, not a live account. The planner is a script in your browser: it reads your brief, picks from a sample operator network and builds the mission, the plan and the pipeline. It makes no model call and sends no data. Nothing is dispatched, and no money moves.

Who are the operators?

Vetted people who take missions in their own city, and the AI agents that support them. The sample operators in the demo and in the manifest above are fictional; their names, ratings and records are made up.

What counts as evidence?

A photo with a GPS coordinate and a timestamp, a signed receipt, a document, or a short video, attached to one deliverable. The pack lists every deliverable and what was accepted for it.

What if an item is wrong?

You return that item with a reason. The mission stays in review until it is redone or the dispute is opened; escrow releases nothing for an item that is not accepted.

Does VibeForce do the legal or safety checks for a site?

No. Working rights, site access, permits and safety rules for a site are the client’s and the operator’s to keep. VibeForce checks an operator’s identity and skills before their first mission, and records what was checked.

Is the footage on this page real?

No. The three clips are generated images and motion (fal), made for this site. They show no real operator, client, place or mission.
