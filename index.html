Brief a mission How it works

The demo runs in your browser. Mission control on this site is a working demo on a sample operator network: a scripted planner, not a model call, and the page sends nothing. Nothing you type leaves your device.

From brief to proof in hours

A mission is a small, well-scoped piece of work in the physical world: a site visit, a supplier check, a delivery, a demo, an event setup.

  1. 01BriefSay what has to happen, where and by when. The planner writes the objective, the deliverables and the acceptance criteria you will check.
  2. 02MatchVibeForce shortlists operators by city, skill and record, with the reason for each pick. You see the rate and the escrow before anyone moves.
  3. 03ExecuteThe operator works the mission and posts photos, GPS and notes as they go. You watch the timeline, not a chat thread.
  4. 04Evidence packThe mission closes with one pack: each deliverable, its evidence, the time it was captured and who captured it.
  5. 05Debrief and payYou accept the pack, or return one item with a reason. Escrow releases the milestones that were accepted, and the operator's record grows.

Questions

Is the demo on this site real?

It is a working demo, not a live account. The planner is a script in your browser: it reads your brief, picks from a sample operator network and builds the mission, the plan and the pipeline. It makes no model call and sends no data. Nothing is dispatched, and no money moves.

Who are the operators?

Vetted people who take missions in their own city, and the AI agents that support them. The sample operators in the demo are fictional; their names, ratings and records are made up for the demo.

What counts as evidence?

A photo with a GPS coordinate and a timestamp, a signed receipt, a document, or a short video, attached to one deliverable. The pack lists every deliverable and what was accepted for it.

What if an item is wrong?

You return that item with a reason. The mission stays in review until it is redone or the dispute is opened; escrow releases nothing for an item that is not accepted.

Does VibeForce do the legal or safety checks for a site?

No. Working rights, site access, permits and safety rules for a site are the client's and the operator's to keep. VibeForce checks an operator's identity and skills before their first mission, and records what was checked.
