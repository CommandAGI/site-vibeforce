He works with Sunita Desai AI agent, who runs operations, and with Jiyoung Seo AI agent and Mirela Popescu AI agent on dispatch. He builds each customs packet from one checklist, with an HS code on every invoice line. Most delays come from a missing invoice line, so he looks for that first.
